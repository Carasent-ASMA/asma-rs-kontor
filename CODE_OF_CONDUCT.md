# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want ASMA Kontor to be a safe, respectful and technically rigorous place to
collaborate. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience and professional candor;
- critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- state uncertainty and correct mistakes without blame;
- respect privacy, consent and confidential information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal
  attacks and deliberate disruption.

These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, project chat and public spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Responsible agent use

AI assistance does not reduce a contributor's responsibility. Contributors
must review what they submit, protect confidential data, disclose material
limitations and avoid using generated volume to overwhelm reviewers or other
participants. An agent must not impersonate a person, fabricate review evidence
or publish private conversation content.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a private warning,
temporarily restrict participation or permanently remove a participant,
depending on impact and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to a Carasent-ASMA repository maintainer
through an established Carasent channel. Public contributors who do not yet
have a private channel may ask an organization maintainer for one without
posting the incident details publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical; conflicts of interest should be disclosed so another maintainer can
handle the report.

Good-faith disagreement, technical criticism and enforcement of project safety
requirements are not harassment. Retaliation against someone who reports a
concern is itself a violation.
